# Brand and offer notes

## Positioning

**AI Workbench San Diego** runs five-person, hands-on workshops for local owner-operators and small-business teams. Participants bring one recurring task and leave with a tested, reusable AI workflow.

## Brand voice

- Practical, calm, local, and specific
- Beginner-friendly without talking down to people
- Outcome-led rather than tool-led
- Honest about risk, limits, and human review

## Core promise

Bring the task that steals your time. In two focused hours, turn it into a workflow you can use again next week—not a folder of lecture notes.

## First event

**AI for Small-Business Owners: Save Five Hours a Week**

- Two hours, in person
- Maximum five participants
- Founding price: $149 total per person
- $49 deposit after the date and fit are confirmed
- Each participant builds on their own laptop with a sanitized example from their real work

## Required takeaways

Every participant identifies:

1. One task to start using AI for
2. One task that should not use AI
3. Estimated weekly time saved
4. Their next automation opportunity

They also leave with a reusable prompt, input checklist, tested output, and human-review checklist.
