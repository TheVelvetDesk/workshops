## Mission Statement
**Title**: The Mission
**Copy**: 
Coding used to be about syntax errors and missing semicolons. Not anymore. 
We believe in **Vibe Coding**: the art of directing AI to build your vision while you stay in the flow. 
But flow is better with friends. 
We exist to bring San Diego's most creative builders out of isolation and into a room where energy is contagious, ideas are amplified, and products actually get shipped.

## How It Works (The "Squad" Model)
**Step 1: The Vibe Check (Application)**
You're not just a resume. We want to know what lights you up. You apply, we vet your skills, your vibe, and your vision.

**Step 2: The Draft**
We curate a "Squad" of 5 compatible builders. Designers, backend wizards, prompt engineers—balanced to build.

**Step 3: The Session**
We host the space. A premium office in SD. High-speed internet, whiteboards, and zero distractions. You show up, meet your squad, and build.

## The Value (Why join?)
- **Curated Network**: No random networking events. Meet people you actually want to build with.
- **IRL Energy**: Zoom calls kill creativity. Physical presence ignites it.
- **Ship Faster**: With a squad and AI, you'll build in a day what used to take a month.
