1. Scope and operator

This notice applies to The Velvet Desk workshop hub, track questionnaires and related communications. The Velvet Desk determines why and how questionnaire information is processed. Third-party websites and AI tools have their own notices.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

We collect the form fields you choose to submit, including contact details, business context, current tools, skills, challenges, workflow descriptions and goals. Optional fields are marked optional.

Information collected automatically

Website hosting and application systems may generate limited technical logs such as IP address, device or browser information, timestamps, requested pages and error or security events. We use this information to deliver and protect the service, not to build advertising profiles.

Sensitive information

Our questionnaires are not designed to collect sensitive or regulated information. Do not include passwords, financial account details, medical records, private customer data, government identifiers, biometric data or confidential documents. If we receive unnecessary sensitive information, we may delete or redact it.

3. How we use information

  • Review and respond to an application or inquiry.
  • Assess which workshop, skill level or private engagement may be suitable.
  • Ask clarifying questions and arrange next steps.
  • Plan curriculum using aggregated or de-identified themes.
  • Protect the site, prevent abuse and troubleshoot submissions.
  • Comply with law, enforce agreements and protect legal rights.

We do not use questionnaire information for materially unrelated purposes without providing an updated notice where required.

4. AI-assisted processing and human review

AI tools may assist a person in organizing, summarizing or drafting notes about an application. A person reviews the application and decides whether and how to respond. We do not use questionnaire responses to make employment, housing, credit, health-care, insurance or other legally significant decisions.

We do not intentionally use identifiable questionnaire responses to train or fine-tune a public or general-purpose AI model. When using a service provider, we apply available business or privacy settings and limit the information provided to what is reasonably necessary.

5. When information is disclosed

We may disclose information to service providers that help us host the site, process form submissions, store application records, send email, maintain security or provide professional services. They receive information only to perform services for us and are expected to protect it.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful process, investigate fraud or security incidents, protect rights and safety, or complete a business reorganization subject to appropriate protections. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Retention and deletion

Applications are retained while they are being evaluated and while follow-up or a business relationship remains reasonably possible. We then delete, de-identify or archive records when they are no longer reasonably necessary, subject to security, dispute-resolution, accounting and legal requirements. Archived records are not treated as active applications and may be permanently deleted from the private dashboard.

7. California privacy rights

The California Online Privacy Protection Act requires commercial websites collecting personally identifiable information from Californians to conspicuously post a privacy policy. The CCPA, as amended by the CPRA, applies to businesses meeting statutory thresholds and provides rights that may include access, correction, deletion, opt-out, limitation and non-discrimination.

Whether a specific CCPA right legally applies depends on the business, processing and applicable exemptions. As a practical privacy commitment, you may ask us to:

  • Confirm whether we hold questionnaire information about you.
  • Provide a copy of information you submitted.
  • Correct inaccurate contact or application information.
  • Delete your application, subject to legitimate legal or security exceptions.

Email thevelvetdeskadvisory@gmail.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject line and the email address used for the application. We may request limited information to verify the request. We will not discriminate against you for making a privacy request.

Your Privacy Choices: We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is no separate sale/sharing opt-out needed for the current questionnaire service. If that practice changes, we will update this notice and provide required choices before the change.

8. Cookies, analytics and browser signals

The questionnaire pages do not currently use advertising pixels or cross-site behavioral advertising. Hosting providers may use essential technologies or logs to deliver and secure the service.

Because there is no uniform “Do Not Track” standard, the site does not separately respond to DNT headers. We do not permit third parties to collect personally identifiable information on these questionnaire pages over time and across unrelated websites for targeted advertising. Since we do not sell or share personal information for behavioral advertising, a Global Privacy Control signal does not require an additional change to that practice.

9. Security, children and limitations

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information involved, including HTTPS in transit, restricted dashboard access, data minimization and the ability to archive or delete applications. No system is completely secure, so do not submit confidential or regulated records.

The questionnaires are intended for adults and business users, not children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child submitted information.

10. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as our services, vendors or legal obligations change. The “Last updated” date identifies the current version. Material changes will be presented on the site before they apply where required.
