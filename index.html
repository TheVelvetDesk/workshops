02Inside the workshop

The tools shaping work right now.

We will compare categories, demonstrate leading tools, and put the most useful ideas into practice throughout the session.

  1. 01

    Text AI & large language models

    ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and other leading models. Learn how they can be used beyond answering everyday questions.

  2. 02

    AI search & research

    Explore Perplexity, NotebookLM, Consensus, and Elicit for deep research, source-based answers, document analysis, and organizing what you find.

  3. 03

    Image AI

    Generate and edit images indistinguishable from reality. Learn how instructions, references, and style choices affect the result.

  4. 04

    Video AI

    See how today’s frontier video generators compare and where AI video can fit into marketing, training, and creative work.

  5. 05

    Voice, audio & music

    Explore voice cloning, conversational AI, and music generation.

  6. 06

    AI coding & app building

    See how a clear idea can become a working website, internal tool, or app live on the internet.

  7. 07

    Automation & AI agents

    You will have access to your own personal assistant that can take action on your behalf. It operates from its own computer and can do almost anything you need it to do digitally. We will help you set it up for day-to-day tasks and show you how to use it responsibly.

  8. 08

    Data & documents

    Use AI to work with PDFs, spreadsheets, reports, and business information.

  9. 09

    Sales, marketing & customer service

    Explore practical workflows you can bring back to your business for lead research, content, outreach, follow-up, and customer support.

Good to know

A few answers
before you ask.

Do I need any AI experience?+

No. This workshop begins with the fundamentals and assumes you are new to the tools.

Will I need to know how to code?+

No. The exercises focus on everyday knowledge work and use conversational AI tools.

Is this a lecture?+

No. Concepts are taught in short sections, followed by live examples and guided practice on your laptop.

How does the hybrid format work?+

You can attend in person at our San Diego venue or join the workshop live online. Connection details and the final venue will be sent before the event.

What happens after I reserve?+

In-person tickets and payment are confirmed through Eventbrite. If you are joining online or want us to tailor the workshop around your goals, complete our short workshop questionnaire and we will follow up by email.

Can you run this workshop for a team?+

Yes. Private sessions can be adapted around your team’s roles, tools, and real workflows.
