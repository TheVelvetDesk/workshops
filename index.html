Straight answers

Before you bring your laptop.

Is this for complete beginners?+

Yes. If you can use email and a web browser, you can participate. The five-person cap gives us time to help everyone individually.

Why is the pilot $149 instead of $300?+

The founding cohort is a market test. You receive the full two-hour workshop at a pilot rate in exchange for candid feedback. Future custom clinics with pre-work and private follow-up will be priced higher.

What should I bring?+

A charged laptop, access to an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, and examples of one repetitive task. Remove private client or employee information before the workshop.

When and where is the first session?+

We are matching the first five applicants by schedule. The application asks for your preferred time; the final central San Diego room and date will be confirmed before any deposit is requested.

What happens after I apply?+

You will receive a fit and schedule confirmation. If the cohort is a match, a $49 payment link reserves your seat and the remaining $100 is due before the workshop.
