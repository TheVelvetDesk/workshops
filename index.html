01The premise

Find what is useful. Then try it yourself.

AI is moving fast. We will move beyond ChatGPT to explore the major categories of modern AI through practical demonstrations and hands-on exercises. You will see how the tools compare, where they fit, and how to bring useful workflows back to your work.

02Inside the workshop

The tools shaping work right now.

We will compare categories, demonstrate leading tools, and put the most useful ideas into practice throughout the session.

  1. 01

    Text AI & leading models

    Work with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and other leading models—and learn why the same request can produce different results.

  2. 02

    Search & research

    Use Perplexity, NotebookLM, Consensus, and Elicit to investigate a question, work from trusted sources, and organize what you find.

  3. 03

    Images, video, voice & audio

    Generate and compare creative work across leading image and video models, then explore voice cloning, conversational AI, music, and audio editing.

  4. 04

    Coding & app building

    See how a clear idea can become a working website, internal tool, or app—even if you do not write code.

  5. 05

    Automation & AI agents

    Understand the difference between a chatbot, an automation, and an agent that can take action. We will help you start setting up an agent for day-to-day tasks.

  6. 06

    Data, documents & business workflows

    Analyze PDFs, spreadsheets, reports, and business information, then apply AI to practical sales, marketing, and customer-service work.

Good to know

A few answers
before you ask.

Do I need any AI experience?+

No. This workshop begins with the fundamentals and assumes you are new to the tools.

Will I need to know how to code?+

No. The exercises focus on everyday knowledge work and use conversational AI tools.

Is this a lecture?+

No. Concepts are taught in short sections, followed by live examples and guided practice on your laptop.

How does the hybrid format work?+

You can attend in person at our San Diego venue or join the workshop live online. Connection details and the final venue will be sent before the event.

What happens after I reserve?+

In-person tickets and payment are confirmed through Eventbrite. If you are joining online or want us to tailor the workshop around your goals, complete our short workshop questionnaire and we will follow up by email.

Can you run this workshop for a team?+

Yes. Private sessions can be adapted around your team’s roles, tools, and real workflows.
