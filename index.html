The Mission

Coding used to be about syntax errors and missing semicolons. Not anymore.

We believe in Vibe Coding: the art of directing AI to build your vision while you stay in the flow. But flow is better with friends. We exist to bring San Diego's most creative builders out of isolation and into a room where energy is contagious, ideas are amplified, and products actually get shipped.
