The premise

Your business is the case study.

Generic demonstrations are easy to forget. In this room, we use a sanitized example from your actual work—an estimate follow-up, weekly update, customer FAQ, content brief, document summary, or another repeatable task—and turn it into a workflow you can run again.

120-minute agenda

Build, test, improve.

00–10

Choose the right task

Score your task for frequency, time cost, risk, and repeatability. Set a clear safety boundary.

10–30

Give AI useful context

Learn a simple briefing structure: role, goal, source material, constraints, examples, and output format.

30–50

Map the workflow

Separate the human judgment from the repeatable steps. Decide exactly where AI helps.

50–95

Build on your laptop

Create and run your reusable prompt, input checklist, and output template with live coaching.

95–110

Test before you trust

Check facts, tone, missing context, and risk. Revise the system based on a second real example.

110–120

Measure the win

Estimate weekly time saved, name one task that should not use AI, and select your next opportunity.
